### $\boxed{№421}$

$$
x^{2}y''=(y')^{2}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{пусть}\quad  & y'=z(x) \\
 & y''=z'
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\text{тогда}\quad x^{2}z'=z^{2}\quad|\cdot dx
$$
$$
x^{2}dz=z^{2}dx\quad|:(x^{2}z^{2})
$$
$$
\frac{1}{z^{2}}\cdot dz=\frac{1}{x^{2}}dx
$$
$$

 \int\frac{1}{z^{2}} dz=\int\frac{1}{x^{2}}dx+C_{1}
$$
$$
-\frac{1}{z}=-\frac{1}{x}+C_{1}\quad|\cdot(-1)
$$
$$
\frac{1}{z}=\frac{1}{x}-C_{1}
$$
$$
\frac{1}{z}=\frac{1-C_{1}x}{x}
$$
$$
z=\frac{x}{1-C_{1}x}
$$
$$
\left[ \begin{aligned}
 & z=\frac{x}{1-C_{1}x} \\
 & z=0
\end{aligned} \right. 
$$
$$
\left[ \begin{aligned}
 & y'=\frac{x}{1-C_{1}x} \\
 & y'=0
\end{aligned} \right. 
$$
#### $\text{I}$
$$
y'=\frac{x}{1-C_{1}x}
$$
$$
y=\int \frac{x}{1-C_{1}x}dx+C_{2}
$$
1) $C_{1}\neq0$
   $$
   \begin{aligned}
   \int \frac{x}{1-C_{1}x}dx & =\frac{1}{C_{1}}\int \frac{C_{1}x-1+1}{1-C_{1}x}dx \\
    & =\frac{1}{C_{1}}\int \left( -1+\frac{1}{1-C_{1}x} \right)dx \\
    & =\frac{1}{C_{1}}\cdot x-\frac{1}{C_{1}^{2}}\ln \lvert 1-C_{1}x \rvert 
   \end{aligned}
   $$
   $$
   y=\frac{1}{C_{1}}\cdot x-\frac{1}{C_{1}^{2}}\ln \lvert 1-C_{1}x \rvert +C_{2}
   $$
2) $C_{1}=0$
   $$
   y=\frac{x^{2}}{2}+C
   $$

#### $\text{II}$
$y'=0$
$y=C$
